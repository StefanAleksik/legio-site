Legio: Gallic Frontier · a real-time tactics game for Android

Legio is in a closed test on Google Play before its launch. Testers play the whole campaign free (all 25 battles, no ads) and help it reach the store: Google lets a new game launch only after testers have kept it installed for 14 days.

You need an Android phone (Android 8 or newer) and the Google account you use in its Play Store. Use that same account in all three steps.

  1. Join the testers’ group

    Open the group and press Join group. It sends no emails; it only tells Google Play you’re a tester.

    Join the group

  2. Become a tester

    Then open the test page and press Become a tester. If it says you aren’t on the list yet, wait a minute after step 1 and reload.

    Become a tester

  3. Install the game

    On your phone, open the store page and install. (Before step 2 this page shows “not found”: that’s normal.)

    Open in Google Play

Please keep it installed for 14 days and play a battle or two. Anything you notice, good or bad, helps: legio.game.support@gmail.com.

Questions

Does it cost anything? No. The test is free, the whole campaign, no ads.

What do you collect? Nothing. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional and only keeps a cloud copy of your campaign and your achievements. See the privacy policy.

How do I leave? The test page from step 2 has a Leave the program link; then uninstall the game.
