Privacy Policy

Legio: Gallic Frontier · effective 3 November 2026

Legio: Gallic Frontier (“the game”) is made by Divomomce (“we”). This page explains what the game does with your information.

What we collect

Nothing ourselves. The game:

  • has no account of its own,
  • shows no ads,
  • contains no analytics, tracking or advertising software of our own,
  • asks for internet access only for Google Play Games, below.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in, Google keeps your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar), a copy of your campaign progress and your achievements in your Google account, so you can carry on on another phone. Google’s Play Games software also collects diagnostics to keep it working. The game asks for internet access only for this. You can play fully without signing in. To delete this data, use your Play Games profile settings or your Google account.

What stays on your phone

Your campaign progress (villages won, your army, gold) and your settings (music and sound volume, picture quality) are stored in the game’s own storage on your phone; only your campaign progress is also copied to your Google account, and only if you sign in to Google Play Games. Uninstalling the game deletes what is on the phone. Android’s own device backup may include them, depending on your phone’s settings; that backup is run by Google under Google’s privacy policy, not by us.

Crash reports

If you have allowed your phone to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google Play may report crashes of the game to us in the Google Play Console. These reports come from Google, describe the crash and the phone model, and contain nothing we can use to identify you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

Later versions may add a one-time in-app purchase. Before it is released, this page will be updated to describe exactly what it involves, and the date above will change.
