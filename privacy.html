Privacy Policy

Legio: Gallic Frontier · effective 23 October 2026

Legio: Gallic Frontier (“the game”) is made by Divomomce (“we”). This page explains what the game does with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. The game:

  • has no account and no sign-in,
  • shows no ads,
  • contains no analytics, tracking or advertising software,
  • does not ask for internet access, so no data leaves your phone through the game.

What stays on your phone

Your campaign progress (villages won, your army, gold) and your settings (music and sound volume, picture quality) are stored only in the game’s own storage on your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes them. Android’s own device backup may include them, depending on your phone’s settings; that backup is run by Google under Google’s privacy policy, not by us.

Crash reports

If you have allowed your phone to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google Play may report crashes of the game to us in the Google Play Console. These reports come from Google, describe the crash and the phone model, and contain nothing we can use to identify you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

Later versions may add Google Play Games sign-in (to keep your campaign in the cloud) and a one-time in-app purchase. Before either is released, this page will be updated to describe exactly what it involves, and the date above will change.

Contact
